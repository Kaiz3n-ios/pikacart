# Unslop: copy rules

Use on any website, app or marketing text. Remove AI tells, keep the facts.

Source: condensed from the unslop skill in pstack by Lauren Tan (MIT) via felipebasurto/unslop-ui (MIT). Keep this credit if you share the rules.

## Content
- **Superficial -ing phrases.** "showing strong momentum", "ensuring a smooth experience", "highlighting key trends". Delete the phrase.

## Language
- **AI vocabulary.** Crucial, delve, enhance, seamless, showcase, pivotal, vibrant, landscape, testament, elevate, unlock, empower. Use the plain word or cut.
- **Fancy ways to say "is".** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Say "is" or "has".
- **"Not just X, but Y."** State the point directly.
- **Rule of three.** Don't force ideas into groups of three. Use the natural number.
- **Synonym cycling.** Workspace, project and space for the same thing. Pick one name and repeat it.
- **False ranges.** "From startups to enterprises" where the ends are not on a real scale. Name who it is for.

## Style
- **Em dashes.** None. End the sentence or use a comma.
- **Title case.** Use sentence case.

## Filler
- **Filler phrases.** "In order to" becomes "To". Delete "It is important to note that".
- **Generic conclusions.** "The future of finance is here." State a fact or cut.

## Plain speech
- **Say what it does, not how it feels.** "Finance, simplified" names a feeling. Name the mechanism or a number. If the sentence could sit unchanged on another product's page, cut it.
- **Shorten or split dense sentences.** One idea per sentence.
- **Active voice.** "Your changes are saved by the editor" becomes "The editor saves your changes".
- **Cut adverbs.** Seamlessly, effortlessly, quickly, significantly. Cut them or give the number.
- **Prefer the plain word.** "Utilize" and "leverage" become "use", "facilitate" becomes "help".
- **Mannered prose.** Aphorisms, rhetorical fragments and figurative verbs. Say the literal thing.

## Hard limits (always)
- Never invent facts, numbers, features or testimonials.
- If a sentence needs a number you don't have, flag it. Do not make one up.
- Leave claims you can't verify unchanged and list them for the client to confirm.
